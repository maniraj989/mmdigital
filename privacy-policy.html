Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy: MM Digital Garage values your trust. This Privacy Policy provides a straightforward explanation of what information is collected when you visit our website or submit an inquiry, and how that information is handled.

1. Introduction

MM Digital Garage ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website located at https://www.mmdigitalgarage.tech. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle personal data collected directly through this Website and during preliminary business communications.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect information that you voluntarily choose to provide to us when contacting our team or requesting a proposal. We do not engage in covert surveillance or unnecessary personal tracking.

3. Information You Provide

When you contact us via direct email, telephone, WhatsApp, or through our website contact form, you may provide us with:

  • Your full name
  • Your business email address
  • Your telephone number
  • Company or brand name
  • Details regarding your project scope, technical requirements, and marketing goals

4. Contact Form Information

Information submitted via our interactive proposal request form is transmitted solely for the purpose of evaluating your inquiry, preparing tailored service recommendations, and responding to your request. We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact form submissions to third-party data brokers or marketing lists.

5. How We Use Information

Any information collected from you is used strictly for legitimate commercial purposes:

  • Responding to your project inquiries and delivering requested quotes or proposals.
  • Communicating with you regarding scheduling, discovery calls, and project updates.
  • Executing agreed-upon digital marketing, web engineering, or creative design deliverables.
  • Maintaining internal business records and administrative accounting.

6. Communication

We may contact you via email or phone in direct response to your outreach. We do not send unsolicited bulk marketing newsletters without your prior consent. You may request to cease communication at any time by replying to any correspondence or contacting us at mmdigitagarage@gmail.com.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our Website is built as a lightweight, privacy-conscious static web application. We use standard browser localStorage solely to remember your preferred visual theme (Light or Dark mode). We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site tracking cookies.

8. Analytics

Standard web server hosting infrastructure may automatically generate basic server access logs (recording technical parameters such as IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, and timestamp) to maintain site security, detect malicious automated requests, and diagnose operational server errors. These logs are stored temporarily and are not merged with personal contact records.

9. Third-Party Services

Our website loads legitimate open assets from trusted Content Delivery Networks (specifically Google Fonts for typography and Cloudflare/cdnjs for Font Awesome icons). These providers receive standard HTTP request metadata necessary to deliver the requested font files and stylesheets to your browser.

10. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical safeguards, including HTTPS SSL encryption across the entire Website, to protect data in transit. While no digital communication method is 100% impenetrable, we apply industry-standard practices to guard against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

11. Data Retention

We retain inquiry information and project communications only for as long as necessary to fulfill the commercial purposes outlined in this policy, manage ongoing client relationships, or comply with applicable legal and financial reporting obligations.

12. Your Privacy Choices

You have the right to request access to the personal contact details you have shared with us, request corrections to inaccurate records, or ask that we delete your inquiry data from our active correspondence systems. To exercise these choices, please email our team.

13. Children's Privacy

Our Website and professional services are intended exclusively for commercial enterprises and adult business professionals. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect technical changes in our services or revisions in relevant regulations. Any updates will be published directly on this page with an updated revision date.
